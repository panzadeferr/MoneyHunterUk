Crypto: the risks

Volatility, no FSCS protection and common crypto scams.

Investing · 5 min read · Last reviewed 11 October 2026

Crypto assets can swing wildly in value, and they have far fewer protections than savings or regulated investments.

The key risks

  • Extreme volatility: prices can fall 50% or more quickly.
  • No FSCS protection if an exchange fails.
  • No Financial Ombudsman route for many complaints.
  • Scams are common: fake exchanges, “pump” groups, fake celebrity ads and romance scams.

If you choose to buy

  1. Only use money you could afford to lose entirely.
  2. Use firms registered with the FCA for crypto activities, and check the FCA Warning List.
  3. Never share wallet recovery phrases. Anyone with them owns your crypto.
  4. Be suspicious of anyone who contacted you first or promises returns.
Watch out

Anyone asking you to pay in crypto, whether a “bank”, “HMRC” or an “investment manager”, is almost certainly a scammer.
