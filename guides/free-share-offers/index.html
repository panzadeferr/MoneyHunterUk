Free share offers

How free-share sign-up offers work and the terms to check.

Investing · 4 min read · Last reviewed 11 October 2026

Some trading apps give you a free share, or cash to invest, when you sign up or refer a friend. It can be a nice bonus, but it's still an investment.

Check before you sign up

  • Is the firm authorised? Check the FCA Register.
  • Minimum deposit or holding period before you can sell or withdraw.
  • The range of possible share values. Most people get the lower end.
  • Fees once the promotion ends.
  • Whether the account is a Stocks & Shares ISA or a taxable account.
Watch out

The value of the share can fall. Don't deposit more than you're comfortable investing just to unlock a bonus.

Tip

Treat the free share as a way to learn how a platform works, not as a reason to start trading frequently.
