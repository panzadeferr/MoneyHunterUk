Council tax & water

Discounts, bands and water meters.

Bills · 4 min read · Last reviewed 11 October 2026

Council tax is based on your home's valuation band, but discounts and exemptions can cut it significantly.

Key points
  • Living alone (only adult): usually 25% off
  • Some people are “disregarded”, for example full-time students
  • Council Tax Reduction can help if you're on a low income

Check your band

If your band looks higher than similar homes nearby, you can check it with the Valuation Office Agency (in England and Wales). Bands can go down, but also up, so research first.

Water bills

  • A water meter often saves money if fewer people live in your home than there are bedrooms.
  • A sudden jump in usage could mean a leak. Contact your water company.
  • Ask about social tariffs if you're struggling to pay.
