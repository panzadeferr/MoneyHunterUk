Common mistakes

The errors that cost hunters their bonuses.

Money Hunter Plans · 5 min read · Last reviewed 11 October 2026

The most common ways hunters lose bonuses or money, and how to avoid them.

  • Not reading the full terms: missing one condition means no bonus.
  • Direct Debits not “active” by the deadline.
  • Paying in from the wrong source (e.g. an internal transfer when the offer needs an external one).
  • Closing an account too early and triggering a clawback.
  • Counting cashback before it's paid.
  • Using voucher codes that void cashback.
  • Stacking credit applications before a mortgage.
  • Letting free trials roll into paid subscriptions.
  • Buying things just because there's a bonus.
  • Trusting “offers” from strangers' links, or messages that pressure you.

The fix

  1. Keep one tracker with every offer, its conditions and deadlines.
  2. Set calendar reminders a week before each deadline.
  3. Confirm details on the provider's own site.
  4. When in doubt, skip it. There's always another offer.
