The State Pension

State Pension age, NI years and tracing lost pensions.

Pensions · 4 min read · Last reviewed 11 October 2026

The State Pension is based on your National Insurance record, not on your earnings.

Key points
  • State Pension age is 66, rising to 67 between 2026 and 2028
  • You usually need 10 qualifying NI years to get any new State Pension, and 35 for the full amount
  • Check your forecast for free on gov.uk

Fill the gaps

  • Check your NI record for missing years.
  • Some gaps are filled by credits (e.g. claiming certain benefits or Child Benefit for a young child).
  • You may be able to pay voluntary contributions to fill gaps. Check whether it's worth it for you first.

Lost pensions

Use the government's free Pension Tracing Service to find old workplace or personal pensions.
