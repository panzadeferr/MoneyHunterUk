Your consumer rights

Refunds, faulty goods and cooling-off periods.

Smart Shopping · 5 min read · Last reviewed 11 October 2026

When you buy from a business, the Consumer Rights Act 2015 gives you rights. You don't need a receipt for these rights to exist, but you do need proof of purchase.

Key points
  • Faulty goods: full refund within 30 days
  • Within 6 months: the seller must repair or replace, and it's assumed the fault was there from the start
  • Online and phone purchases: usually 14 days to cancel (cooling-off), with some exceptions

Your rights in plain English

  • Goods must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described.
  • Services must be carried out with reasonable care and skill.
  • Refurbished and second-hand goods from a trader still carry rights, allowing for their age and condition.
  • Personalised items, perishables and unsealed hygiene items are usually excluded from the 14-day cooling-off.

Extra protection when you pay

Credit card purchases of £100 to £30,000 may be covered by Section 75. Debit card purchases may be recoverable through chargeback.

Tip

Extended warranties at the checkout are often poor value. Check what the law and the manufacturer already cover first.
