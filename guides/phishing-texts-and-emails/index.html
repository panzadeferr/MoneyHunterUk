Phishing texts & emails

Spotting fake messages and links.

Scam Defence · 5 min read · Last reviewed 11 October 2026

Phishing is a fake text, email or call designed to get your details or your money. It's the most common way scams start, and once you know the pattern, they become easy to spot.

The 6 red flags

  • Urgency: “within 24 hours”, “account locked”, “final notice”.
  • Surprise money: refunds, rebates and prizes you didn't expect.
  • Links to lookalike addresses (e.g. a “gov-uk-refund” site rather than one ending in gov.uk).
  • Requests for card details, passwords, PINs or one-time codes.
  • Generic greetings: “Dear Customer”.
  • Odd payment methods: gift cards, crypto, bank transfer to a personal account.

Safe habits

  1. Never use the link or number in an unexpected message.
  2. Open the official app, or type the address yourself.
  3. Check delivery, tax and bank issues inside your real accounts.
  4. If in doubt, ask someone you trust. Scammers rely on you acting alone and fast.
Tip

A padlock in the browser only means the connection is encrypted. Scam sites can have padlocks too.
