Subscription clear-out

Finding and cancelling forgotten subscriptions.

Budgeting · 4 min read · Last reviewed 11 October 2026

Forgotten subscriptions are one of the easiest savings you'll ever find. A single £12.99 monthly subscription costs £155.88 a year.

The 20-minute clear-out

  1. Open your banking app and search for recurring payments (many apps list them).
  2. Check your app store subscriptions on your phone too.
  3. List each one: cost per month, last time you used it.
  4. Cancel anything you haven't used in the last month.
  5. Downgrade or share plans where allowed. Rotate streaming services month by month instead of holding them all.

Free trial traps

  • Read the price after the trial and how to cancel before you enter card details.
  • Set a reminder a day before the trial ends.
  • Be wary of trials that can only be cancelled by phone during office hours.
Tip

Once a year, call your providers (phone, broadband, insurance) and ask for a better deal. Many will offer one to keep you.
