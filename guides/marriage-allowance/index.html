Marriage Allowance

Transferring allowance between partners.

Tax · 3 min read · Last reviewed 11 October 2026

If you're married or in a civil partnership, and one of you earns less than the Personal Allowance, you may be able to transfer some allowance and save tax.

Key points
  • Transfer £1,260 of Personal Allowance to your partner
  • Saves up to £252 a year
  • Can be backdated up to 4 years

Who qualifies

  • The lower earner has income below the Personal Allowance (£12,570).
  • The higher earner is a basic-rate taxpayer.
  • You're married or in a civil partnership.

How to apply

Apply for free on gov.uk. You don't need a company to do it for you. Some firms charge a cut of your refund for something you can do in minutes.
