Buy Now Pay Later

How BNPL works and how to avoid its traps.

Credit & Borrowing · 4 min read · Last reviewed 11 October 2026

Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) splits a purchase into instalments, often interest-free. It feels painless, which is exactly why it's easy to overdo.

Key points
  • Agreements taken out from 15 July 2026 are regulated by the FCA
  • Lenders must check you can afford it
  • You can complain to the Financial Ombudsman, and Section 75 refunds may apply
  • Agreements from before that date are not covered

The risks

  • Stacking several plans at once and losing track.
  • Late fees, and missed payments being passed to debt collectors.
  • Missed payments can affect your credit file.
  • Spending more than you would with money you actually have.

Use it safely

  1. Only use BNPL for things you could afford to pay for today.
  2. Keep a list of every plan and its payment dates.
  3. Pay from an account that always has the money in it.
  4. Avoid BNPL for everyday essentials. If you need it for food, talk to a free debt adviser.
