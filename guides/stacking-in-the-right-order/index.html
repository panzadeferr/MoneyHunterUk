Stacking in the right order

What to click, and when, so your cashback actually tracks.

Cashback · 4 min read · Last reviewed 15 October 2026

Most stacking mistakes come from doing things in the wrong order. This is the routine TopCashback and Quidco describe in their own help pages.

  1. Decide what you're buying and whether you'd buy it anyway.
  2. Compare prices first, then do the rest of the steps below. Comparison sites visited after your click can break tracking.
  3. Open the cashback site, or its app or extension, and check the shop's terms: rate, exclusions, and whether gift cards or voucher codes are allowed.
  4. Start with an empty basket and one shop tab open.
  5. Click through from the cashback site, then buy in the same session.
  6. Pay with the card you chose, and keep the order confirmation.

Things that break tracking

  • VPNs, ad blockers and privacy software.
  • Two cashback or voucher extensions at once.
  • Clicking through too early, then browsing other sites.
  • Several shop tabs open at once.
  • Reusing one click for a second order: each order needs its own click.
Tip

TopCashback says that if your cashback hasn't tracked after 7 days, you can lodge a missing cashback claim. Check the current guidance on their help page.

Watch out

Help pages change. Treat this as a checklist and check the cashback site's own page for the shop before you buy.
