Selling your stuff

Getting the best price and staying safe.

Side Income · 4 min read · Last reviewed 11 October 2026

Selling things you no longer need is one of the quickest ways to raise cash, and selling your own belongings usually isn't taxed.

Sell faster, for more

  1. Price it using recently SOLD listings, not asking prices.
  2. Take clear photos in daylight from several angles.
  3. Write an honest description, including flaws.
  4. Compare postage options, or offer local collection.
  5. Reply quickly and be polite. Good reviews help you sell again.

Stay safe

  • Only accept payment inside the platform, or in cash when meeting.
  • Never click a buyer's link to “receive payment”.
  • Never refund an “overpayment”.
  • Meet in public places for collections.
Tip

If you start buying things to resell for profit, that's trading. Check the £1,000 trading allowance and HMRC rules.
