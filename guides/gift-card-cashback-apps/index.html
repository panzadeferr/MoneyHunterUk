Gift card cashback apps

Discounted gift cards and their risks.

Cashback · 4 min read · Last reviewed 15 October 2026

Gift card apps sell retailer gift cards at a discount or with cashback. You then spend the card as normal and keep the saving.

Best practice

  1. Check the cashback rate for your shop just before you buy, as rates move.
  2. Buy only the amount you'll spend soon.
  3. Pay with a card that also earns rewards, but clear any credit card in full.
  4. Spend the gift card, and withdraw the cashback from the app regularly.

Risks

  • Unused balances can be lost if a retailer goes into administration.
  • Some gift cards expire. Check the date.
  • Gift cards are rarely refundable once bought.

What can go wrong

  • If a retailer goes into administration, it can stop accepting gift cards or limit how much of an order they cover.
  • Cards often expire one to two years after purchase, and some charge inactivity fees. Read the terms.
  • Which? found in 2021 that 38% of people holding vouchers for failed retailers couldn't spend the full balance (survey from October 2021, so check current terms).
Tip

If a retailer looks to be in trouble, spend the card quickly. If you paid by credit card, you may be able to make a Section 75 claim with your card provider.

Watch out

Anyone who asks you to pay a bill, fine or tax in gift cards is a scammer. No real organisation does this.
