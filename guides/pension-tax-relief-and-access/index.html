Pension tax relief & access

Tax relief, access age and the tax-free lump sum.

Pensions · 5 min read · Last reviewed 11 October 2026

Pensions get tax relief on the way in, which makes them powerful for long-term saving. In exchange, you can't normally touch the money until later in life.

Key points
  • Annual allowance: £60,000 of tax-relieved contributions a year (lower for some high earners and people who've started drawing a pension)
  • Normal minimum pension age: 55, rising to 57 from 6 April 2028
  • Usually up to 25% can be taken tax-free, capped by the lump sum allowance (£268,275)

How tax relief works

Contributions get relief at your income tax rate. Basic-rate relief is often added automatically, and higher-rate taxpayers may need to claim the extra through Self Assessment.

Compounding over decades

£100 a month growing at 5% for 30 years becomes about £83,000, from £36,000 paid in.

Watch out

Offers to unlock your pension before the minimum age usually mean huge tax charges, or that the money is stolen.
