Who we are

Money Hunters UK ("we", "us") is a free, independent deal-tracking website at moneyhunters.co.uk. We list UK cashback, bank-switch, referral and sign-up offers so you can find them in one place. We are not a bank, cashback provider or financial adviser, and we are not affiliated with any of the companies whose offers we list. Questions? Email hello@moneyhunters.co.uk.

Affiliate disclosure

Some links on this site are affiliate or referral links. If you click one and sign up or make a purchase, we may earn a commission from the provider. This never changes the offer you receive and never costs you extra. It is how we keep the site free. Offers are independently listed and commissions never influence how we present a deal. This site is not financial advice: always verify the terms of any offer directly with the provider before signing up.

What we collect

We keep data collection to a minimum:

  • Newsletter sign-ups. If you subscribe to our weekly email, your email address is stored by Brevo, our email provider, so we can send it to you. You can unsubscribe from any email with one click.
  • Usage data. Like most websites, our host records standard technical logs (pages visited, browser type, approximate region) for security and performance.
  • We do not ask for your name, address or payment details, and we never see your passwords.

Cookies & tracking

We do not set advertising cookies ourselves. When you click through to a provider, that provider (or its affiliate network, such as Awin, Skimlinks or Sovrn) may set its own cookies to track that the referral came from us. Those cookies are governed by that provider's own privacy policy. Your browser's standard cookie settings can block or clear these at any time.

Third parties we use

  • Brevo, to deliver our newsletter (double opt-in).
  • GitHub Pages, to host the site.
  • Affiliate networks (e.g. Awin, Sovrn/Skimlinks), who track clicks that leave our site.

You can ask us to access, correct or delete any personal data we hold about you (in practice, that is just your newsletter email address). Email us at hello@moneyhunters.co.uk and we will respond within 30 days. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
