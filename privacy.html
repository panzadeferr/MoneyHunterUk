Affiliate disclosure

Some links on this site are affiliate or referral links, and each one is labelled Ad. If you sign up or buy through one, we may earn a commission from the provider. It never costs you extra and never changes what we list or the order we show it in. Always check an offer's terms on the provider's own site before signing up.

What we collect, why, and how long we keep it

  • Weekly email (optional). If you subscribe, your email address is stored by Brevo, our email provider, so we can send the weekly email. We only add you after you tick the consent box and confirm by email (double opt-in). Every email has an unsubscribe link. Legal basis: your consent. Kept until you unsubscribe.
  • Account (optional). If you create an account in the app, we store your email address, a password (handled by our login provider; we never see it), your username, your app progress (such as points and streaks) and the offers you choose to track, including their steps, status and expected payout amounts and dates. This lets you use the offer tracker on any device. Your username can appear on the leaderboard. Stored with Supabase, our database and login provider. Legal basis: providing the service you asked for. Kept while your account is active and deleted 12 months after your last sign-in, or sooner if you ask.
  • Anonymous usage statistics. We use Umami Cloud to count visits and a few actions, such as starting the game, opening a guide or opening an offer. Umami does not use cookies, and we do not send it your name, email or account details. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the site.
  • Technical logs. Our host (GitHub Pages) and the content network that delivers part of the app's code (jsDelivr) receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type when pages load, for security and performance. These are kept by those providers under their own policies.

We do not ask for your address or payment details, and we do not sell your data.

Data stored on your device

To work without an account, the app and the game save some information in your browser's local storage on your own device: your game save and chosen player name, app settings, your progress and tracked offers, and, if you typed them into a form, your first name, email address and username. This stays on your device; we cannot see it unless you create an account. The app also uses a service worker so it opens quickly and works offline. You can delete all of this at any time by clearing your browser's site data for moneyhunters.co.uk.

Cookies & tracking

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use advertising pixels (for example from Meta, TikTok or Google). Our statistics are cookieless. If you sign in, our login provider keeps you signed in using your browser's storage; this is strictly necessary for the account to work. When you follow a link to a provider, that provider may set its own cookies, which are covered by its own privacy policy.
