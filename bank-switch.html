Banks pay new customers a cash bonus to move an existing current account across using the Current Account Switch Service (CASS). The switch takes 7 working days, moves your balance, direct debits and cards automatically, and the old account closes. Most people use a spare "dummy" account so their main banking setup never changes. Bonuses below are the ones live today, with the main conditions.

How to stack the most from this

  • One bonus per person, per bank. Space switches a few months apart and check each bank's own exclusion rules before applying; a failed eligibility check wastes a hard credit search.
  • Set up the direct debits early. Offers like Lloyds' only count direct debits that were active on your old account before the switch, not ones you add afterwards.
  • Switching uses a credit check. A small, temporary dip in your credit score is normal.

Always read the full terms on the bank's own page before applying. This list is checked against current sources (Moneyfacts, Be Clever With Your Cash, Money To The Masses, Which?) but offers can be withdrawn at any time.
